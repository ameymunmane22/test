# Incident Management Policy
Version 2.1 | Effective: 2026-03-01

## Incident Severities
- **SEV-1 (Critical)**: Total system outage. Paging the on-call engineer immediately.
- **SEV-2 (High)**: Core features broken for a large subset of users.
- **SEV-3 (Low)**: Minor cosmetic issues or slow queries.

## Communications
Updates will be posted to `status.nexora.com`.
