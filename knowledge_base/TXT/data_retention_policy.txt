Data Retention and Privacy Policy
Version 1.0 | Effective: 2024-06-01

Data Privacy
Nexora adheres to GDPR and CCPA. We do not sell your personal data.

Data Retention
To minimize data footprints, user logs, API request logs, and audit trails are retained for a maximum of 30 days.
