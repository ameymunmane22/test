Billing and Payment Policy
Version 2.0 | Effective: 2025-12-01

Payment Methods
We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and wire transfers for Enterprise accounts.

Invoices
Invoices are generated on the 1st of every month and are due Net 30.

Refunds
Customer satisfaction is our priority. Refunds are processed if requested within 30 days of billing.
