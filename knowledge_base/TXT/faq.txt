Frequently Asked Questions
Version 2.1 | Effective: 2026-05-01

Q: How do I reset my API key?
A: Go to Settings -> API Keys and click "Regenerate".

Q: What is the refund policy?
A: Refunds must be requested within 14 days of the original purchase. After 14 days, no refunds will be processed.

Q: Can I change my account email?
A: Yes, navigate to Profile -> Personal Information.
