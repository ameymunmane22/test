Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Version 2.0 | Effective: 2025-06-01

Uptime Commitment
Nexora guarantees 99.9% uptime for core API services. 

Service Credits
If uptime falls below 99.9% in a given month, customers are eligible for a 10% service credit.
